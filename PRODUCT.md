# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Haitian youth, local communities (such as in Gonaïves), and the Haitian diaspora who want to connect, register for local events, watch live streams, and discover influential voices shaping the country's future.

## Product Purpose
Ayibuzz Media is a digital hub designed to showcase, connect, and elevate the next generation of Haitian creators, speakers, and cultural events. It serves as an active portal for event registrations, speaker profiles, and live stream broadcasts.

## Brand Personality
Patriotic, vibrant, youth-centric, modern, and inspiring. Guided by the motto "Leve ansanm, Briye ansanm" (Rise together, shine together).

## Anti-references
Generic corporate event portals, dry SaaS dashboard templates, and uninspiring blog templates.

## Design Principles
1. **Celebrate the Culture**: Leverage curated brand colors, high-impact imagery, and bold visual landmarks that resonate with the Haitian spirit.
2. **Vibrant & Alive**: Bring pages to life with smooth micro-interactions, responsive flex layouts, and modern typography contrast.
3. **Information Accessibility**: Ensure that event details, registrations, and live stream states are scannable and instantly clear.
4. **Seamless Mobile-First Layout**: Provide a responsive experience that works beautifully on varying mobile connections and screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance with high-contrast text ratios (≥4.5:1), clear click targets, keyboard navigation support, and reduced-motion stylesheet fallbacks.
